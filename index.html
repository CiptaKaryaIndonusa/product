About Our Company

PT Cipta Karya Indonusa is an export company committed to providing top-quality products from Indonesia to meet the needs of the global market. We specialize in supplying processed biomass products, such as wood pellets and wood chips, and also offer premium charcoal and briquettes to satisfy global demand. With a steadfast commitment to sustainability and rigorous quality control, we ensure that our international buyers receive exceptional biomass solutions for energy and power generation.
